Window marking inspection

Bird-safe gap check

Find the gaps in a bird-safe window treatment before the job is signed off.

How to take the photo
  1. Tape the printed sheet flat on the glass, beside the markings.
  2. Keep the whole sheet and the marked area in frame.
  3. Stand so the sheet fills about a third of the photo width, roughly square-on (under 40 degrees).
  4. Avoid the sun's reflection on the dots, and use good light: a dark photo cannot be read.
